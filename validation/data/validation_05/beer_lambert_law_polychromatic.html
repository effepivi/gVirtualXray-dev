Beer-Lambert Law, Polychromatism Case

The results presented here have been generated using validation_05.cxx. In this test, we will simulate the geometry as follows:

  • A cube, which has edge length of 3 cm, is made of soft tissue (HU ≈ 52).
  • Inside, a cylinder is inserted. It is made of bone (HU ≈ 1330), its height is 3 cm and its diameter is 2 cm.

The incident energy beam is define as follows:

The absolute error is:

|I'o - Io| = |4.353E+00 MeV - 4.359E+00 MeV| = 0.006 MeV

|I''o - I'o| = |4.353E+00 - 4.353E+00| = 0.000 MeV
